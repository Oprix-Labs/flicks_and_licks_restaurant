The Origin Story

Born from a Love of
Cinema & Craft

In 2019, two obsessive food lovers — a former film director and a Michelin-trained chef — set out to create something that had never existed before: a restaurant that felt like stepping inside a film.

The name says it all. Flicks for the dramatic, atmospheric world of cinema — brooding lights, heightened emotion, storytelling that grips the soul. Licks for the primal joy of extraordinary food, a flavour so good you simply cannot stop.

Together, they designed every element of the restaurant: the arched ceilings, the velvet banquettes, the table spacing that feels intimate yet spacious. And then they handed the kitchen to one of Britain's most gifted culinary talents, Chef Isabelle Moreau, who transforms seasonal British produce into edible theatre every single night.

What We Stand For

Our Pillars

Fresh Ingredients

Every ingredient is sourced from trusted local artisan producers. We change our menu seasonally — sometimes weekly — to honour what nature provides at its peak.

Cinematic Ambiance

Our dining room is a set — designed by award-winning interior architects to create a mood that shifts from the warmth of aperitif hour to the dramatic depth of late-night dining.

Unforgettable Flavours

Every dish is engineered to create a memory. Our chefs study the psychology of taste, pairing textures and temperatures to create moments that you'll still be talking about years later.

Our Journey

The Story So Far

2019

Doors Open

Flicks & Licks opens its doors at 12 Cinematic Square, Soho. Within six weeks, we're fully booked three months in advance.

2020

Resilience & Reinvention

Navigating global challenges, we launch our first at-home tasting experience — gourmet delivery kits that bring the Flicks & Licks magic to homes across the UK.

2021

First Michelin Nomination

Chef Moreau's kitchen receives its first Michelin Guide nomination. The Evening Standard names us "Restaurant of the Year."

2023

The Private Dining Room

We unveil The Projection Room — a private dining experience for up to 12 guests, complete with a bespoke film screening curated by Dorian Ashby.

2025

A New Chapter

Expansion of the bar programme with our acclaimed cocktail architect David Roux, three further Michelin nominations, and a new tasting menu launching this autumn.
